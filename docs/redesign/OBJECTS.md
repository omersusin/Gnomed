## Where to put the app's mark on an object (boxes for `fit()`)

| helper | gives |
|---|---|
| `envelope_stamp(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) of the printable area on envelope()'s stamp - fit() the mark into it. |
| `browser_page(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) of browser_window()'s page - fit() the mark into it (anchor="bottom"). |
| `bottle_label(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) of bottle()'s white label - fit() the mark into it. |
| `bag_front(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) of shopping_bag()'s front - fit() the mark into it. |
| `goal_ring_centre(cx, base, size)` | (x, y) of goal_ring()'s hole centre - put a ball there. |
| `record_label(cx, base, size, sleeve=True)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) inside vinyl_record()'s round label - fit() the mark into it. |
| `cassette_label(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) of the printable area on cassette()'s label - fit() the mark into it. |
| `speaker_badge(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) of the badge area at the top of speaker() - fit() the mark into it. |
| `headphone_cup(cx, base, size)` | (x0, y0, x1, y1) on headphones()'s right ear cup - fit() the mark into it (small: use for a simple, bold mark; a detailed mark needs vinyl_record / cassette / speaker instead). |

Example: `bottle(ic, 64, 118, 54, ramp=RAMP["Blue"])` then
`for g, c in zip(fit(MARK, bottle_label(64, 118, 54)), COL): solid(ic, g, face=c, depth=0, top=False)`.
A white mark on a white label/stamp vanishes: print it in the brand colour (Telegram's stamp: the plane in blue).
`MARK`/`COL` come from `regions(SLUG)` (the concept template does this): a gradient logo's bands
come back as ONE part with a real gradient (`Grad`) - never `colour()` per band (docs/PLAYBOOK.md#gradients).

Ramps: `Blue`, `Green`, `Yellow`, `Orange`, `Red`, `Purple`, `Brown`, `Light`, `Dark`
