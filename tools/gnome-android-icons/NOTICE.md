These files are **study references** for building new artwork offline. Copying
their paths into your own icons creates a derivative work under their licence.
Brand names and logos of third-party apps belong to their owners; this skill
ships no third-party brand artwork. MoreWaita and Adwaita++ are not bundled
(read from your local archives by `scripts/community_refs.py`).
