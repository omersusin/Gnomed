  2. the pupil (a green disc in the almond) with the sclera around it
  3. the neon brand green #80eb12

  an AI agent that sees - the eye itself is the object (a protective amulet)

  B (L2 a GNOME object that carries the mark): a green glass loupe, the spiral as its handle
